Beauty Calendar

Organize your beauty products with ease.

Calendar

We offer a fully functional calendar designed to manage your daily schedule while providing specialized features for your beauty routine. With the Beauty Calendar, you can easily log purchase and expiration dates for your makeup and skincare products, allowing you to track daily usage and ensure your routine stays fresh and effective.

Logging Products

Logging your collection is as simple as a single click. Just tap the Make-Up or Skin-Care text and enter the product name and brand. While adding the purchase and expiration dates is optional, doing so unlocks our smart database features. We'll provide you with community-based estimations on how long your product will last and send you a timely notification when it's time for a restock.

Statistics

Unlock deep insights into your beauty habits with our Pro Version. Visualize your routine through personalized statistics that highlight your most-used essentials and identify products gathering dust. Discover your all-time favorites and track evolving usage trends to make smarter, more sustainable beauty choices.

Schedule Recommendations

Experience personalized skincare coaching with our Pro-exclusive Ingredient Intelligence. Our system analyzes the products you own to recommend the perfect routine for your specific skin concerns. Plus, stay safe with our advanced compatibility checker: we'll alert you to ingredient combinations that shouldn't be used together, ensuring your skin stays radiant and irritation-free.
