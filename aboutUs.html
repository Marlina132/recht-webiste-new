Why our brand exists

We are a group of friends who had a combined struggle, constantly losing track of our beauty products, buying ones we already had, or misusing others. After a late-night conversation we came to the conclusion, we can't just continue wasting our time like that. So we sat together and created this website. At first it was just us four who used it, but then the idea came to us that there are probably many other people living through the same struggles that we did. As a result we reached out to professionals and in collaboration with them, we created the "Beauty Calender" and made its core features free! This way every person can get a little bit more organized. :D

Meaning

With our brand we stand for femininity, self-care and empowerment. It is important to us that each and every person consuming our product is satisfied and feels fulfilled.
